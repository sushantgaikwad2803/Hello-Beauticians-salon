About Us – Hello Beauticians

Your trusted partner for premium salon services at the comfort of your home

Welcome to Hello Beauticians, your trusted partner for premium salon services at the comfort of your home. We are a women-focused home salon service based in Pune, dedicated to delivering professional beauty care with convenience, hygiene, and personalized attention.

At Hello Beauticians, we believe that every woman deserves to feel confident, beautiful, and pampered—without the hassle of traveling to a salon. That's why our expert beauticians bring high-quality services right to your doorstep, ensuring a relaxing and luxurious experience in your own space.
